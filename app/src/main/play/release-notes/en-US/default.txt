An identifier too long for the navigator to read is now refused as you type it,
rather than reaching the panel as a route through a different place. Waypoints,
airways, procedures and transitions are checked against the model's own limits,
and a refusal says what is wrong.

The application is now in French as well as English.

The device list shows Garmin devices only, says how many it hid, and gives one
tap back to all of them.
