SendFPL

A flight plan, from your phone to the panel

SendFPL sends a flight plan from an Android phone straight to a navigator in the panel over Bluetooth, so you do not have to type it in on the unit. The navigator holds it for preview, and you open its catalog page and accept it.

  • Type a route, or open one your planner exported: Garmin .fpl, GPX, KML, .pln, SkyDemon's .flightplan or a .gfp. Share it from another app, or open it from any file manager.
  • User waypoints and coordinates.
  • Shows the exact route string before it goes.
  • English and French.
  • Works offline: no account, no network permission, nothing leaves your phone.

Supported navigators

GNC 355, GNX 375, GPS 175 and GTN 6xx/7xx. Each model's limits come from reading its own flight plan parser, and a model that has not been read is left out rather than given another's numbers. The first three are one firmware family under a single product id, and the bench testing was done on a unit from it. The GTN is supported from its software, not from a unit on a bench.

Open source

SendFPL is free and open source under the MIT licence, at github.com/0intro/sendfpl.

Before you fly

SendFPL is not certified avionics. The navigator holds an imported plan for preview. Check every waypoint there before you accept it. The pilot in command is responsible for the plan flown.
