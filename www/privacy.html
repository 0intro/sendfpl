SendFPL

Privacy policy

This policy covers the SendFPL Android application, package app.sendfpl, published by David du Colombier.

SendFPL collects nothing and sends nothing anywhere. The app has no network permission at all, so it cannot.

What it keeps, on your phone only

  • The Connext credential it uses to authenticate to a navigator, stored encrypted and excluded from backup.
  • The Bluetooth address of each navigator you have uploaded to, and when.

The route itself is not in that list. It is held in memory while the app is open and is never written anywhere, so closing the app is enough to be rid of it.

None of this leaves the device. There are no servers, no accounts and no analytics, so there is nothing to request or delete. Uninstalling the app removes everything it stored.

Bluetooth

SendFPL uses Bluetooth to reach a navigator you have already paired. The BLUETOOTH_SCAN permission is declared with neverForLocation: your position is never derived from it.

The one thing that ever leaves your phone is the flight plan itself, when you press Send. It goes over Bluetooth to the navigator you picked, which is a device you own and have already paired. It is not sent to the developer, and there is nowhere else for it to go.

Questions

Questions about this policy, or about anything the app holds, go to David du Colombier, 0intro@gmail.com. Bugs and feature requests are better raised at github.com/0intro/sendfpl/issues.
